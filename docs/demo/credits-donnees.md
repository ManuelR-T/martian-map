# Data credits (#17)

Credits and conditions of use of the data displayed in the demo. They are copied from `apps/web/public/data/manifest.json` and `packages/data/src/siteSheets.ts`. This data does not constitute NASA or USGS support for this project.

| Data | Credit | Terms of use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MOLA colorized relief, global map (463 m/pixel) | NASA / MGS MOLA scientific team; distributed by NASA Mars Trek | NASA content reusable without prior permission for factual use, without suggesting endorsement; cite NASA as the source ([NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) rules). |
| Jezero HiRISE mosaic (0.25 m/pixel) | NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona; distributed by NASA Mars Trek | HiRISE images are in the public domain, with credit requested ([HiRISE](https://uahirise.org/media/usage.php) policy). Trek Mosaic Specific Conditions: Unconfirmed. |
| Altitudes, slopes and shaded relief HiRISE (4 m/pixel) | USGS Astrogeology Science Center; NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona, March 2020 Terrain Relative Navigation HiRISE DTM Mosaic | No access constraints; cite Fergason et al., 2020 ([USGS](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_2020_terrain_relative_navigation_hirise_dtm_mosaic) file). |
| Altitudes, slopes and shaded relief CTX (20 m/pixel) | USGS Astrogeology Science Center; NASA/JPL, March 2020 Terrain Relative Navigation CTX DTM | No access constraints; cite Fergason et al., 2020 ([USGS](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_2020_terrain_relative_navigation_ctx_dtm_mosaic) file). |
| Perseverance Pickup Points: Skinner Ridge, Wildcat Ridge, Amalik | NASA/JPL-Caltech, March 2020 Perseverance, sampling map (mars.nasa.gov) | Same NASA rules as the MOLA relief. |
| Photos of the sites (sols 495, 516 and 575) | NASA/JPL-Caltech; [NASA catalog of samples](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/mars-rock-samples/) | Same NASA rules as the MOLA relief. |

Site sheet texts are based on NASA JPL ([2022](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-perseverance-rover-investigates-geologically-rich-mars-terrain/), [2023](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/videos/meet-the-mars-samples-shuyak-and-mageik-samples-14-and-15/)), NASA Sample Catalog, and [USGS on Apollo 17](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/documentation-and-environment-apollo-17-samples) samples.

Reference to cite for reliefs: Fergason, R. L. et al., 2020, *March 2020 Terrain Relative Navigation Flight Product Generation: Digital Terrain Model and Orthorectified Image Mosaics*, 51st Lunar and Planetary Science Conference ([PDF](https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2020/pdf/2020.pdf)).
